A note on the island nav:

6 icons (Home + 5 domains) is workable but on the denser side for a floating pill — real iOS-style islands usually top out around 4-5 comfortably. Options below.

1

Keep all 6 in the island

Home + 5 domain icons, slightly smaller touch targets. Simple, everything one tap away.

2

Home + 4 domains + "More"

Island shows Home, your top 4 domains, and a "More" icon that expands a sheet for the rest. Roomier targets.

3

Home + hub icon only

Island just has Home and a single "Domains" icon that opens a grid/menu to pick which domain. Cleanest island, one extra tap to reach a domain.
