Design notes

Why this works

  • One tap serves double duty: logs the check-in AND signals kill-list progress
  • "Other work" bucket keeps necessary-but-not-priority tasks from inflating your Noise count
  • Ratio format (4.2:1) is more motivating/legible than a raw percentage
  • Timeline dots on the home card give an at-a-glance shape of your day before you even open analytics

Still your call

  • Missed check-ins are shown as excluded (not auto-noise) — flip this if you want accountability pressure instead
  • Check-in window (e.g. 8am–8pm, every 2hrs = 6/day) needs a start/end time you set
