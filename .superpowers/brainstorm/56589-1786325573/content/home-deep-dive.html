Why each layer earns its space

What this solves

  • Hero eliminates "what do I do right now" decision fatigue
  • Pulse rings give the full-picture status your morning/night review actually needs
  • Time-grouped list (not domain-grouped) matches how urgency actually works across mixed domains
  • Everything checkable inline — zero navigation to log a prayer or tick a kill-list item

Trade-offs to be aware of

  • More visual complexity than a flat list — worth it only if you'll actually use the hero/pulse, not just the list
  • Business kill-list items need to "explode" into the list individually or roll up as one line (shown rolled-up here)
  • Fitness/School are light-touch here since they're checkmark-simple — most of the visual weight naturally goes to Deen + Business
